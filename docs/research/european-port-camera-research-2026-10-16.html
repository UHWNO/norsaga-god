Recommendation

Start the next integration review with Riga and Hamburg. Riga offers eight official public streams; prioritize Sea Gate, Krievu Island and the port panorama for broad maritime coverage without adding every similar viewpoint. Hamburg's Teufelsbrueck image shows container terminals and shipping traffic. Both match formats already supported by the app. Operator reuse permission and actual map-marker positions must still be confirmed before release.

This is a broad screening survey, not a claim that every European port has a public webcam or that all discovered cameras are alive. “No vetted feed” means no source met the evidence needed in this pass; it does not mean a camera cannot exist. Camera pages and search listings are weaker evidence than direct, repeated media checks. Existing app cameras are identified separately and were not automatically revalidated.

Scope: port basins, terminals, harbor mouths and maritime approaches only. Seagoing ports such as Hamburg and Antwerp remain relevant even though they are on rivers. Inland city streets, road networks, beaches without a useful port view, ski cameras, virtual tours and AIS-only maps are excluded from proposed additions. No existing inland cameras were removed.

What the current viewer can use

The app already supports direct image snapshots and clear MPEG-TS HLS streams. Its HLS proxy rejects encrypted streams, fragmented MP4, byte ranges and media that leaves the registered origin. A YouTube, Feratel, Skyline, Streamdays or other embedded webpage is not itself a compatible direct feed. No new viewer or camera code was written for this research.

Direct technical checks

  • Riga: all eight playlists returned HTTP 200, advanced sequence numbers between samples and returned video segments. The app's actual HLS downloader and parser accepted all eight; full downloaded TS segments were approximately 0.8–1.7 MB, below the current 4 MB segment cap. This is a transport / format check, not a production viewer deployment or an audit of every camera's scene.
  • Hamburg Teufelsbrueck: HTTP 200 image/jpeg; repeated images differed and showed a current date. The app's actual snapshot fetcher accepted the image. A visual inspection confirmed the container-port / shipping-channel view.
  • Rostock: two current images, but neither supplied Content-Type in the inspected responses. The unchanged app snapshot fetcher returned no usable frame. This needs a narrowly scoped adapter or provider header correction.
  • Le Havre: master, variant and media were reachable. The stream uses fragmented MP4 / EXT-X-MAP; the app parser rejected it as unsupported.
  • Helsinki: tested official Vuosaari and West Harbour JPEGs were unchanged; Last-Modified was October 14 and October 9 respectively. Those two stills fail the freshness requirement. Separate official video cameras were not validated.
  • Other holds: Genoa and Valencia direct images returned 403; Constanta's disclosed HLS URL returned 404. Gdansk's operator says its live cameras are suspended. No access restrictions were bypassed.

API and permission implications

No new paid API key is technically needed to fetch the nine tested public Riga / Hamburg media URLs. A public page does not itself establish a redistribution license. Confirm the operator's permitted use, credit and refresh rate before adding a source. Keep original credits and label still images, delays and approximate camera positions honestly.

Windy's Webcams API can help discover cameras by country, category and location and requires an API key. Its metadata, preview and timelapse access is not a guarantee of a licensed, fresh live-video feed for this viewer. It is optional for discovery, not necessary for the small verified shortlist. Skyline's FAQ limits live-video embedding to webcam hosts, so those listings are permission holds. Stale or expiring player URLs should never be copied as permanent feeds.
